## Difficulty guidelines

- **Easy:** Establishes the main rocket type or geometry.
- **Medium:** Adds a measurable design constraint, such as length, apogee, or motor type.
- **Hard:** Adds the remaining parameters or a more detailed configuration.

The apogees and motor specifications are carried over from the supplied list and have not been independently re-simulated. A motor name alone does not guarantee a particular apogee; the result also depends on the complete design, mass, drag, and simulation settings. These prompts are not verified flight-ready designs.
