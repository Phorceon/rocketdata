## Builder bugs

No confirmed bug in `sftgen/orkbuild.py` or `sftgen/tools.py` was found: every large divergence traced back to a converter loss or to the reference file itself, not to the builder. Verified by direct JPype comparison of converted vs original docs: per-stage structural masses match to <1 g, motor ignition/separation event sequences match, fin axial placement is correct, and deploy events carry over per configuration.

Converter bugs found and fixed while iterating (in `sftgen/convert_old.py`):

- `'auto <value>'` radii in .ork XML are STALE computed values; OpenRocket re-resolves them from neighbours on load. Using them literally produced phantom diameter steps (fixed by neighbour inheritance).
- Fin sets mounted on `<transition>` elements were silently dropped (fins now re-hosted on the nearest body tube at the same absolute x).
- Several `fincount=1` sets at the same axial position are authors' way of writing an N-fin pattern with custom angles; converting each literally left overlapping sets and broke OpenRocket's Barrowman CP (they are now merged).

Non-bug divergences to keep in mind:

- `tools.simulate` seeds the PinkNoise wind model; `or_headless.simulate` runs the file's own (unseeded) conditions, so windy sims differ randomly.
- `stability_cal` (or_headless: first valid CP-CG over bounding diameter) and `stability_rail_exit_cal` (builder: stability time series at rail exit over reference diameter) are different measures; treat them loosely.
- For files whose motors are absent from the bundled DB (custom thrust curves: Eugene_mk3, GragasMAY24, L839, E17, ...) OpenRocket itself drops the motor (`empty MotorInstance`) and the reference sim is degenerate (apogee 0). These are marked unsupported.

Schema limitations that blocked faithful conversion:

- No parallel boosters / pod sets (11 files), tube fins (3), >4-motor clusters (3), >3 stages (2), stages without a motor mount (passive dart/payload stages, ~8 files).
- Minimum-diameter mounts where motor OD ~= tube ID cannot be expressed (mount wall adds >= 0.4 mm).
- Per-component/per-stage mass & CG overrides are approximated by a single ballast item per stage (CG matched when the ballast fits inside a tube); when the override is LOWER than the converted structure it cannot be applied ('converted structure heavier' notes).
- No deploy events 'lowerstageseparation'/'launch'/'never'; no separation events 'upperignition'/'launch'/'never'; approximated and noted.
- No inner tubes without motors, couplers, bulkheads, engine blocks, launch lugs, rail buttons, shock cords, fin tabs, fin angle offsets, 1-fin sets, fin root chord < 10 mm — dropped, clamped, or merged; mass folds into the ballast correction.
- Stage-boundary and intra-stage diameter steps require a real transition component in the schema; short conical bridges are inserted (noted) and add a small amount of drag/mass.
- Custom (non-catalog) motors are unresolvable: mark unsupported or resolve by thrust-curve digest when the digest is in the DB.
