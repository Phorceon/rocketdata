An AeroTech M1297W in an 84 mm fiberglass rocket, nose plus a 1.55 m tube, with a 2.0 m chute at apogee.
It leaves a 1.5 m rail at 26.7 m/s, holds 1.61 cal, and lands at 5.3 m/s.
Apogee is still 5160 m, about 11% under 19,000 ft, after three revisions. Smaller fins or more impulse would be the next move.
