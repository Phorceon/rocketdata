1. Understand: The only stated goal is apogee about 19,000 ft. 19000 * 0.3048 = 5790 m, and "about" means stay within 5%, so the band is roughly 5500 to 6080 m. Motor, diameter, payload, and flight time are all free. Rail, wind, and landing speed are not given, so I assume a 1.5 m rail, 2 m/s wind, a vertical launch, and the usual 6 m/s touchdown limit. One stage unless the impulse budget forces a second.
2. Feasibility: With no drag, burnout speed for 5790 m is sqrt(2 * 9.81 * 5790) = 337 m/s. A real airframe throws away a large share of that energy to drag, so a mid-power motor will not get there. I want a K or an L, and I need real impulse and diameter numbers before I pick a tube.
