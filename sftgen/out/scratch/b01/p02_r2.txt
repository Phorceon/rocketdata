2. Feasibility: The K550W is 1624.9 N*s in a 54 mm case and weighs 1.487 kg, but its only delay is 0 s, so it cannot coast. The L850W and L1150R are both about 3500 to 3700 N*s and 3.67 kg, and both are 75 mm cases. The Cesaroni 2765L730-P is 2763.2 N*s, 739.1 N average, 54 mm by 0.649 m, loaded mass 2.247 kg, and it has no ejection delay at all, so recovery has to be electronic. On a guessed 3.2 kg liftoff, 2763.2 / 3.2 = 864 m/s of ideal speed against the 337 m/s the altitude needs with no drag. That leaves a large budget for drag. The K is the same diameter with barely half the impulse, and the 75 mm motors buy impulse by getting fatter and heavier. The L730 is the one to try first.
3. Size: Liftoff mass guess 3.2 kg, motor included. Thrust-to-weight = 739.1 / (3.2 * 9.81) = 23.5. Rail acceleration (739.1 - 3.2 * 9.81) / 3.2 = 221 m/s^2, so exit speed sqrt(2 * 221 * 1.5) = 25.7 m/s, over the 15 m/s floor even before the thrust spike. Burnout mass is about 3.2 - 1.35 = 1.85 kg. A 5 m/s descent at Cd 0.8 needs 2 * 1.85 * 9.81 / (1.225 * 0.8 * 5^2) = 1.48 m^2, and sqrt(4 * 1.48 / 3.1416) = 1.37 m. A 1.5 m canopy sits a little under the 6 m/s limit. The case is 0.054 m, so a 1.2 mm phenolic wall makes the mount 0.054 + 2 * 0.0012 = 0.0564 m; I will use 0.057 m so the fit is not a knife edge. A 62 mm fiberglass tube, 0.062 m with a 1.6 mm wall, has room for that mount.
4. Draft: Ogive fiberglass nose 0.25 m on a 1.2 m fiberglass body, 0.062 m outside diameter, wall 0.0016 m. Phenolic mount 0.057 m by 0.66 m, wall 0.0012 m, 10 mm overhang, motor plugged. Three fiberglass fins, root 0.18 m, tip 0.08 m, span 0.1 m, sweep 0.09 m, 3 mm thick, set 0.01 m forward of the tail. A 0.04 kg altimeter and a 1.5 m ripstop chute deploy at apogee. Flying it on the default 1.5 m rail and 2 m/s wind.
