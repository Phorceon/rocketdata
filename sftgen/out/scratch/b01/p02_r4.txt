The M1297W is the useful step up: 5413.8 N*s, 1275 N average, 75 mm by 0.665 m, loaded mass 4.637 kg, and only a 0 s delay, so it stays plugged with an apogee chute. Impulse ratio 5413.8 / 2763.2 = 1.96. The old airframe was about 3.55 - 2.25 = 1.3 kg; a wider tube might make the new liftoff about 6.2 kg, and 6.2 / 3.55 = 1.75. Ideal speed goes from 2763.2 / 3.55 = 778 m/s to 5413.8 / 6.2 = 873 m/s, and (873 / 778)^2 = 1.26, so 4471 * 1.26 = 5630 m before the extra drag of the fatter tube. That is still short of 5790 m on paper, but close enough to fly. Thrust-to-weight 1275 / (6.2 * 9.81) = 21.0, and rail exit sqrt(2 * 196 * 1.5) = 24.2 m/s where (1275 - 6.2 * 9.81) / 6.2 = 196 m/s^2. The case is 0.075 m, so the mount goes to 0.079 m outside with a 1.5 mm wall, and the body and nose grow to 0.084 m with a 2 mm wall. Mount length 0.68 m still fits the 1.2 m tube.
