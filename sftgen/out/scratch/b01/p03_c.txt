Three Estes C6 stages, booster and middle with a 5 s delay, in 22 mm cardboard with burnout staging and a chute on each stage.
With 0.05 kg in the nose, 4.47 m/s wind, and the 2.44 m rail, apogee is 615 m, inside roughly 2,100 ft, and the margin stays positive.
It still fails the pad speed: 13.5 m/s off the rail against 15 m/s. The sustainer also lands at 4.6 m/s, just over 15 ft/s. Less nose weight clears the rail and climbs to about 740 m.
