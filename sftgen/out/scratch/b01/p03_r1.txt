1. Understand: Three stages, motors fixed in firing order. The booster and the middle stage are each an Estes C6-5, so the delay is 5 s, and the sustainer is a C6 with the delay left open. Apogee roughly 2,100 ft: 2100 * 0.3048 = 640 m, within about 5%. Landing is a maximum of 15 ft/s, and 15 * 0.3048 = 4.57 m/s, on every section. Wind about 10 mph: 10 * 0.44704 = 4.47 m/s. Rail is fixed at 8 ft: 8 * 0.3048 = 2.44 m. Launch angle and site altitude are not stated, so vertical and sea level.
2. Feasibility: Only the booster is lit on the pad, so thrust-to-weight has to carry all three stages. A drag-free climb to 640 m needs sqrt(2 * 9.81 * 640) = 112 m/s. Three C6s can do that only if the stack is light, and the same lightness is what the 3:1 thrust rule demands. I need the C6 impulse, thrust, mass, and diameter before I commit to a tube.
