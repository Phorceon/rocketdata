5. Fix: The failing value that explains the rest is the burn margin of -0.69 cal. Burning the tail motor walks the CG forward, and 1.04 cal at the rail is not enough reserve. Upper fins are also sitting ahead of the booster fins and pulling CP forward. I am enlarging only the booster fins, to root 0.095 m, tip 0.04 m, span 0.065 m, sweep 0.045 m, and shrinking the middle fins to root 0.03 m and span 0.016 m and the sustainer fins to root 0.04 m and span 0.022 m. Same motors, tubes, chutes, wind, and rail. If it then flies straight, altitude will be the next problem, because 0.0993 kg is a very light stack for 26.4 N*s.
