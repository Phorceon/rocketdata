5. Fix: The failing number is -0.99 cal, and the 96.2 m apogee is just the pitch-over. A 0.10 kg rocket with a 2.88 cal margin weathercocks in 10 mph, and the booster's 0.0108 kg of propellant is a tenth of the rocket, so the CG walks forward by several calibers as it burns. I am doing two things that are the same fix: cut the booster fins back to root 0.08 m, tip 0.035 m, span 0.048 m, sweep 0.035 m, and put 0.03 kg of nose weight at the top of the sustainer tube. That raises mass toward 0.13 kg, where 4.8 / (0.13 * 9.81) = 3.76, still above 3, and it shortens the CG travel. Middle fins go to root 0.048 m and span 0.03 m, sustainer fins to root 0.05 m and span 0.032 m, so the upper stack has a chance once the booster is gone. Wind and rail stay 4.47 m/s and 2.44 m.
