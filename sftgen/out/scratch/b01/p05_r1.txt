1. Understand: Apogee must clear 2,500 ft, a minimum. 2500 * 0.3048 = 762 m. "Keep it simple" means one stage, one motor, a single chute, no clustering. No wind, rail, or landing number is given, so I assume 2 m/s wind, a 1.5 m rail, vertical launch, and a 6 m/s landing limit. A first scratch build should stay with cardboard, balsa, and a single-use motor if one can make the altitude.
2. Feasibility: Drag-free speed for 762 m is sqrt(2 * 9.81 * 762) = 122 m/s. A D motor on a light rocket usually stops well short of that, and an E is the maybe. I want the impulse of an E and a couple of F motors before I pick a tube.
