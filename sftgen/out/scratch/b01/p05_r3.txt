4. Check: The simulator gives 766.1 m, which clears the 762 m minimum, with 4 m to spare. Rail exit 16.0 m/s, thrust-to-weight 9.4, and landing 3.4 m/s all pass, and the chute opens at 11.5 s, just after apogee at 10.47 s, at 8.7 m/s. The failure is stability: 0.02 cal at the rail and the same 0.02 cal through the burn. Liftoff mass is 0.158 kg, and 0.103 kg of that is the F15 sitting on the tail. The fins cannot get CP behind a CG that far aft.
5. Fix: The failing value is 0.02 cal. A heavier fin would also add drag, and 766.1 m has almost no spare over 762 m, so I am not growing the fins. I am swapping to the AeroTech F44W-8: 42.2 N*s but only 0.048 kg, 24 mm by 0.07 m, delay 8 s. The mount shrinks to 0.026 m outside, wall 0.6 mm, length 0.08 m, so the lighter motor still fits. Airframe, fins, and chute stay as they are. The CG has to move forward, and the rocket gets lighter, which is the direction that protects the altitude minimum.
