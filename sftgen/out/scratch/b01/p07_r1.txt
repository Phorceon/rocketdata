1. Understand: Apogee about 275 m, so the band is roughly 261 to 289 m. Flight time is a maximum of 90 s, and the reason is a short range window, so the clock really does matter. Recovery is a single chute, not a streamer and not dual deploy. Rail, wind, and landing speed are not stated: 1.5 m rail, 2 m/s wind, vertical, and 6 m/s touchdown.
2. Feasibility: sqrt(2 * 9.81 * 275) = 73 m/s with no drag, which is a C motor on a small rocket. The 90 s cap is the part that fights a big chute. From 275 m, a 5 m/s descent takes 275 / 5 = 55 s, plus a climb of about 8 s, so a moderately small chute fits the window and still stays under 6 m/s. I need the C6 numbers to size it.
