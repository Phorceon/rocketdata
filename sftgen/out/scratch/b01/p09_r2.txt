2. Feasibility: J350W and the HP-J425R are both about 670 N*s in a 38 mm case, near 0.65 kg, and both burn in about 1.5 s. The J350W has no delay, so the chute has to be electronic. I300T is only 436.8 N*s, light for 3,000 m. K250W is 2553 N*s and 2.21 kg with a 9.31 s burn, which will overshoot unless the rocket is big. I will try the J350W in a slim tube first. If it falls short, the K is the step up.
3. Size: Guess 1.1 kg. Thrust-to-weight 445.4 / (1.1 * 9.81) = 41, so the 1.5 m rail is easy. Descent mass about 1.1 - 0.36 = 0.74 kg. At 5 m/s, 2 * 0.74 * 9.81 / (1.225 * 0.8 * 5^2) = 0.59 m^2, diameter sqrt(4 * 0.59 / 3.1416) = 0.87 m. A 1.0 m chute covers the 6 m/s limit. The case is 0.038 m, so the mount is 0.0405 m with a 1 mm wall and the tube is 0.044 m.
4. Draft: Fiberglass ogive 0.18 m on a 0.75 m cardboard tube, 44 mm, wall 1.2 mm. Phenolic mount 0.0405 m by 0.35 m. J350W plugged, 0.05 kg altimeter, 1.0 m ripstop chute at apogee. Three fiberglass fins, root 0.13 m, tip 0.05 m, span 0.065 m, sweep 0.06 m, 3 mm thick. Default wind and rail.
