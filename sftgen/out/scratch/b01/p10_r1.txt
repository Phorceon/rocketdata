Rail length is not stated, so 1.5 m. Drag-free speed for 7,500 m is sqrt(2 * 9.81 * 7500) = 384 m/s.

The hard part is getting both pieces under 5.49 m/s, which means a real canopy on each stage, and passing 4 m/s wind plus the 1,300 m site.
