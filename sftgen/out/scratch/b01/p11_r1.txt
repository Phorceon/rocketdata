"L3 flyer" means the team can fly an M, N, or O, not that the motor class is pinned. Drag-free speed for 8,000 m is sqrt(2 * 9.81 * 8000) = 396 m/s, so this is a big motor, not a cluster of Gs.
2. Feasibility: An L or M on a slim tube has been landing nearer 4 km in this catalog's impulse range, so I want something in the O class, or an O with a small upper stage if one motor falls short.
