1. Understand: At least 8,000 feet is a floor, not a band. 8000 * 0.3048 = 2438 m. It has to be on the ground within 4 minutes, 4 * 60 = 240 s from liftoff. Dual deploy is required. Wind is about 5 mph: 5 * 0.44704 = 2.24 m/s, and that goes on every run. Rail and site are not stated, so 1.5 m and sea level. Landing still has to be at or under 6 m/s once the main is open. Drag-free speed for 2,438 m is sqrt(2 * 9.81 * 2438) = 219 m/s.
2. Feasibility: A motor that coasts to 4 or 5 km will eat the 240 s even on a small drogue. I want a J that clears 2,438 m with a little room and no more. Drogue at apogee, main low, so the fall is fast and the last part is slow enough to land under 6 m/s.
