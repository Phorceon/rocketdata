2. Feasibility: J350W is 669.2 N*s, 445.4 N, 1.49 s, 38 mm by 0.337 m, 0.651 kg, and it lists no delays, so dual deploy has to be electronic. HP-J425R is almost the same impulse, 676.8 N*s, with a 14 s delay that would fire a charge we do not want. J135W is 1042.3 N*s in a 54 mm case and would climb well past the floor and eat the 240 s. On a guessed 1.1 kg rocket the J350 thrust-to-weight is 445.4 / (1.1 * 9.81) = 41.3.
3. Size: Drogue at 15 m/s needs 2 * 1.1 * 9.81 / (1.225 * 0.8 * 15^2) = 0.098 m^2, diameter sqrt(4 * 0.098 / 3.1416) = 0.35 m. Main at 5 m/s needs 2 * 1.1 * 9.81 / (1.225 * 0.8 * 5^2) = 0.88 m^2, diameter sqrt(4 * 0.88 / 3.1416) = 1.1 m. A 2,500 m fall on the drogue is about 2500 / 15 = 167 s, plus a short burn and the last 150 m under the main, which should fit in 240 s if the apogee stays near the floor.
