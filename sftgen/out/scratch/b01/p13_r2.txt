2. Feasibility: Estes D12 is 16.8 N*s, 10.4 N, 1.61 s, 24 mm by 0.07 m, 0.0426 kg, delays 0, 3, 5, and 7 s. The C6 is only 8.8 N*s and the E12 is 27.1 N*s, which is more rocket than 400 m wants. On a guessed 0.08 kg airframe, thrust-to-weight is 10.4 / (0.08 * 9.81) = 13.3. A 7 s delay ejects at 1.61 + 7 = 8.6 s, near a small-rocket apogee. The 24 mm case fits a tube well under 55 mm.
3. Size: For about 0.09 kg at 4.5 m/s the chute is 2 * 0.09 * 9.81 / (1.225 * 0.8 * 4.5^2) = 0.089 m^2, diameter sqrt(4 * 0.089 / 3.1416) = 0.34 m. I will use 0.36 m.
4. Draft: Cardboard ogive 0.12 m on a 0.50 m cardboard tube, outer diameter 0.041 m, wall 1 mm. D12 with a 7 s delay in a 0.028 m mount. Chute 0.36 m at ejection. Three birch fins, root 0.06 m, tip 0.03 m, span 0.04 m, sweep 0.03 m, 3 mm. Site altitude 300 m.
