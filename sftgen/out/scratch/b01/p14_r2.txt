2. Feasibility: Estes B6 is class B, 4.3 N*s, 5.2 N, 0.83 s, 18 mm by 0.07 m, 0.0156 kg, and the only delay is 0 s, so the chute has to be electronic or it opens at burnout. The B4 came back as class C, 5.1 N*s, so it is over the cap. The A8 is class A, 2.2 N*s, half of the B. On a guessed 0.04 kg rocket, thrust-to-weight is 5.2 / (0.04 * 9.81) = 13.3.
3. Size: For 0.045 kg at 4 m/s the chute is 2 * 0.045 * 9.81 / (1.225 * 0.8 * 4^2) = 0.056 m^2, diameter sqrt(4 * 0.056 / 3.1416) = 0.27 m. I will use 0.30 m. Tube 0.024 m outer, mount 0.020 m, clear of the 0.018 m case and nowhere near a large airframe.
4. Draft: Cardboard ogive 0.07 m on a 0.28 m cardboard tube, outer diameter 0.024 m, wall 1 mm. B6 plugged, 0.30 m chute at apogee. Three birch fins, root 0.055 m, tip 0.025 m, span 0.04 m, sweep 0.025 m, 3 mm. Sea-level defaults.
