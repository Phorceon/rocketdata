2. Feasibility: Estes D12 is class D, 16.8 N*s, 10.4 N, 1.61 s, 24 mm by 0.07 m, 0.0426 kg, delays 0, 3, 5, and 7 s. That is exactly on the 24 mm cap and on the D cap. The C6 is class C, 8.8 N*s, 18 mm, and would also be legal, but a D still fits under a 457 m ceiling if the rocket is not a pencil. On a guessed 0.12 kg rocket, thrust-to-weight is 10.4 / (0.12 * 9.81) = 8.8. A 7 s delay ejects at 1.61 + 7 = 8.6 s. Five degrees of rail will shave a little altitude off a vertical flight.
3. Size: For 0.13 kg at 4.5 m/s the chute is 2 * 0.13 * 9.81 / (1.225 * 0.8 * 4.5^2) = 0.13 m^2, diameter sqrt(4 * 0.13 / 3.1416) = 0.41 m. I will use 0.45 m. A little nose mass, 0.015 kg, keeps a D from being all tail.
4. Draft: Cardboard ogive 0.11 m on a 0.45 m cardboard tube, outer diameter 0.041 m, wall 1 mm. D12 with a 7 s delay. Chute 0.45 m at ejection. Nose mass 0.015 kg at 0.03 m. Three birch fins, root 0.08 m, tip 0.035 m, span 0.06 m, sweep 0.035 m, 3 mm. Launch angle 5 degrees.
