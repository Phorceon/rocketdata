2. Feasibility: G40W is class G, 99.0 N*s, 46.3 N, 2.13 s, 29 mm by 0.124 m, 0.123 kg, delays 4, 7, and 10 s. G80T is 135.6 N*s, more likely to bust 850 m. F32T is 56.7 N*s and would sit well under, but a G is still legal and is the motor a new flyer would actually buy for this. On a guessed 0.25 kg rocket, thrust-to-weight is 46.3 / (0.25 * 9.81) = 18.9. A 10 s delay ejects at 2.13 + 10 = 12.1 s.
3. Size: For 0.28 kg at 4.5 m/s the chute is 2 * 0.28 * 9.81 / (1.225 * 0.8 * 4.5^2) = 0.28 m^2, diameter sqrt(4 * 0.28 / 3.1416) = 0.60 m. I will use 0.65 m. Tube 0.041 m, mount 0.032 m, clear of the 0.029 m case. Fins a bit large so 2.78 m/s of wind does not weathercock a marginal rocket.
4. Draft: Cardboard ogive 0.14 m on a 0.55 m cardboard tube, outer diameter 0.041 m, wall 1 mm. G40W with a 10 s delay, chute at ejection. Three birch fins, root 0.10 m, tip 0.045 m, span 0.07 m, sweep 0.045 m, 3 mm. Wind 2.78 m/s.
