1. Understand: About 450 m means 428 to 473 m. One chute. It has to land within 800 m of the pad, and the wind is 5 m/s, so that wind goes on every run. Rail, site, and landing speed are not stated: 1.5 m, sea level, and 6 m/s. Drift is the horizontal miss, not the touchdown speed. A 450 m fall at 5 m/s takes 450 / 5 = 90 s, and 90 * 5 = 450 m of drift if the wind just carried it the whole way, so 800 m is roomy if the chute is not huge.
2. Feasibility: A D has been short of 450 m once it is stable. An E is the class to try. The wind is strong enough that an overstable fin set will weathercock and walk off downrange.
