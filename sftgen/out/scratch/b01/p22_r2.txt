2. Feasibility: Cesaroni 7579M1520-P is class M, 7579.1 N*s, 1557.6 N, 4.86 s, 98 mm by 0.548 m, 6.718 kg with 3.737 kg of propellant. It lists no delays, so the chute is electronic. 7579.1 N*s is under the 40,960 N*s cap. On a guessed 10 kg rocket, thrust-to-weight is 1557.6 / (10 * 9.81) = 15.9. A 3 m rail helps the exit speed. About a third of the mass is propellant, so the fins have to be large or the margin dies in the burn.
3. Size: For 10 kg at 5 m/s the chute is 2 * 10 * 9.81 / (1.225 * 0.8 * 5^2) = 8.0 m^2, diameter sqrt(4 * 8.0 / 3.1416) = 3.2 m. I will use 3.4 m so the landing stays under 6 m/s and a coast of a few kilometres still reaches the ground. Tube 0.110 m, mount 0.102 m, clear of the 0.098 m case.
4. Draft: Fiberglass ogive 0.35 m on a 1.15 m fiberglass tube, outer diameter 0.110 m, wall 3 mm. M1520 plugged, 3.4 m chute at apogee. Mount 0.102 m by 0.58 m. Three fiberglass fins, root 0.28 m, tip 0.12 m, span 0.18 m, sweep 0.12 m, 6 mm. Wind 2 m/s, rail 3 m.
