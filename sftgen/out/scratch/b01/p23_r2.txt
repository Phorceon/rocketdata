2. Feasibility: Estes E12 is class E, 27.1 N*s, 11.2 N, 2.41 s, 24 mm by 0.095 m, 0.0599 kg. A 6 s delay is on the card. Ejection is at 2.41 + 6 = 8.4 s. 3. Size: For 0.14 kg at 5 m/s the chute is 2 * 0.14 * 9.81 / (1.225 * 0.8 * 5^2) = 0.11 m^2, diameter sqrt(4 * 0.11 / 3.1416) = 0.37 m. I will use 0.40 m. Nose mass 0.025 kg, because an E in a light tail has been under 1 cal without it.
