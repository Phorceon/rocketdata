2. Feasibility: I200W is class I, 326.8 N*s, 188.4 N, 1.73 s, 29 mm by 0.333 m, 0.3575 kg, and the only delay is 0 s, so the chute has to be electronic. I300T is 436.8 N*s in a 38 mm case, and I284W is 590.8 N*s. The I200 is the closest step up from an H that was nearer 1,200 m. On a guessed 0.60 kg rocket, thrust-to-weight is 188.4 / (0.60 * 9.81) = 32.0.
3. Size: For 0.60 kg at 5 m/s the chute is 2 * 0.60 * 9.81 / (1.225 * 0.8 * 5^2) = 0.48 m^2, diameter sqrt(4 * 0.48 / 3.1416) = 0.78 m. I will use 0.85 m. Tube 0.038 m, mount 0.032 m. A little nose mass, because an I in the tail needs it.
4. Draft: Cardboard ogive 0.15 m on a 0.70 m cardboard tube, outer diameter 0.038 m, wall 1 mm. I200W plugged, 0.85 m chute at apogee. Nose mass 0.02 kg. Mount 0.032 m by 0.35 m. Three birch fins, root 0.11 m, tip 0.05 m, span 0.08 m, sweep 0.05 m, 3 mm. Sea-level defaults.
