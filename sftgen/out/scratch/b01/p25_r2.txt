F50 is 76.8 N*s and G25W is 117.5 N*s, both legal on impulse but more likely to bust 610 m. On a guessed 0.15 kg rocket, thrust-to-weight is 34.2 / (0.15 * 9.81) = 23.2. An 8 s delay ejects at 1.65 + 8 = 9.65 s.
3. Size: For 0.15 kg at 4.5 m/s the chute is 2 * 0.15 * 9.81 / (1.225 * 0.8 * 4.5^2) = 0.15 m^2, diameter sqrt(4 * 0.15 / 3.1416) = 0.44 m. I will use 0.48 m. Tube 0.041 m, wider than the motor, so there is some drag under the ceiling. Nose mass 0.012 kg for the wind.
