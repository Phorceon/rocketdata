1. Understand: Apogee at least 2,500 m (minimum). Motor class at most M (fixed ceiling). Landing speed at most 6 m/s (maximum). Wind about 4 m/s, so the flight will be run at 4 m/s. Rail length, launch angle and site altitude are not stated: I assume a vertical 1.5 m rail at sea level. Nothing asks for staging, a payload, a duration cap or a drift limit, so this is a single-stage altitude rocket with one recovery device.

2. Feasibility: A drag-free coast to 2,500 m needs burnout speed sqrt(2*9.81*2500) = 221 m/s. Drag will eat a large fraction of that, so the airframe has to stay narrow and the motor has to be a long-burn K or L rather than a short, heavy M. An M is legal but usually lives in a 75 mm case, which costs altitude. I need impulse, average thrust and loaded mass for a few 54 mm candidates before I pick one.
