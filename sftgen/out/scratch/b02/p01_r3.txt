4. Check: The simulator gives an apogee of 3420.6 m, which clears the 2,500 m minimum by a wide margin. Landing is 4.5 m/s against a 6 m/s maximum, rail exit is 30.5 m/s against 15 m/s, and thrust-to-weight is 26.6 against 3. The chute opens at 19.5 s, right at apogee. The flight fails the stability gate: margin at rail exit is 0.24 cal, and the minimum through the burn is the same 0.24 cal, below the 1.0 cal launch requirement. Liftoff mass came out 2.06 kg, a bit under my 2.2 kg guess, which is why the altitude is high. There is a supersonic warning (peak Mach 1.87); that does not fail a gate, but the drag model is less trustworthy up there.

5. Fix: The failing value is the 0.24 cal rail-exit margin, limit 1.0 cal. The K550W sits in the tail, so the CG is aft and the first fin set is too small to put the CP behind it. One change: four plywood fins instead of three, with root 0.22 m, tip 0.08 m and span 0.14 m. Motor, chute and tube stay as they are. Altitude has spare room above 2,500 m, so the extra fin drag is acceptable.
