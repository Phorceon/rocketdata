4. Check: The simulator gives 327.3 m, short of 457 m. (457 - 327.3) / 457 = 28% low, outside the 5% band. Flight time is 89.5 s, under the 115 s floor of the 115 to 125 s window. That short duration is mostly the low apogee: landing is already 3.9 m/s, and there is less sky to fall through. Stability passes at 1.13 cal, rail exit 22.9 m/s, thrust-to-weight 9.4, landing under 6 m/s. Ejection at 8.7 s is a little after the 7.32 s apogee. Liftoff mass is 0.113 kg, close to the 0.12 kg guess, so the miss is drag from the 42 mm tube.

5. Fix: The failing values are apogee 327.3 m and flight time 89.5 s. One change: slim the airframe from 42 mm to 34 mm, wall 0.8 mm, and match the nose. Frontal area falls to (0.034 / 0.042)^2 = 0.655. Motor, fins and the 0.39 m chute stay. A lighter, narrower rocket should climb higher and, at a similar sink rate, spend longer in the air.
