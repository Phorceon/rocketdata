5. Fix: The 29 mm D12 cannot serve both windows at once. Apogee is 415.5 m, still under 457 m, while flight time is already 127.3 s, over the 125 s cap, because the 0.39 m chute sinks at only 3.4 m/s. Raising this airframe further would make the clock worse. The E12 has 27.1 N*s against the D12's 16.8 N*s. On the earlier 42 mm D12 flight the apogee was 327.3 m; 27.1 / 16.8 = 1.61 times the impulse, with a heavier motor, should land that fatter tube near 457 m instead of pushing the 29 mm rocket well past it. Last revision, taken together because the two misses pull opposite ways: back to a 42 mm tube, E12-8, mount lengthened to 0.10 m so the 0.095 m case fits, chute opened from 0.39 m to 0.42 m, and the fins enlarged slightly (span 0.065 m) so the heavier motor does not sink the 1.13 cal the 42 mm D12 had.
