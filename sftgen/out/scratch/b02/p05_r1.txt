1. Understand: Apogee about 575 ft, within 5%. 575 * 0.3048 = 175 m. Recovery is a single chute (fixed: one parachute, not a streamer and not dual deploy). Landing under 6 m/s (maximum). Wind, rail and motor are not stated. I assume 2 m/s wind, a 1.5 m vertical rail and sea level. Single stage.
