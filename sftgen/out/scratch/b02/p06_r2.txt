2. Feasibility: Each C6 is 8.8 N*s, 4.8 N average, 14.1 N peak, 1.83 s burn, 18 mm, mass 0.023 kg. Delays include 5 s, which matches the booster the request named. Together that is 8.8 * 2 = 17.6 N*s. For about 229 m I want a fat 42 mm stack, not a minimum-diameter one. Only the booster is burning on the rail. At a guessed 0.12 kg liftoff, thrust-to-weight is 4.8 / (0.12 * 9.81) = 4.08, over 3. Weight is 0.12 * 9.81 = 1.18 N, so average rail acceleration is (4.8 - 1.18) / 0.12 = 30.2 m/s^2 and speed off the 3.66 m rail is sqrt(2 * 30.2 * 3.66) = 14.9 m/s. That average is just under 15 m/s, but the 14.1 N spike covers the first metre of a 3.66 m rail.
