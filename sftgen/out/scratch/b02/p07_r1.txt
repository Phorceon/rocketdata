1. Understand: Apogee at least 250 m (minimum). Landing softer than 4.5 m/s (maximum). Total impulse under 40 N*s (maximum, strict). Rail is 1 m (fixed). Wind and launch angle are not stated: I assume 2 m/s wind, vertical, sea level. Single stage. A 1 m rod is short, so the motor has to be punchy as well as under 40 N*s.

2. Feasibility: Drag-free speed for 250 m is sqrt(2 * 9.81 * 250) = 70 m/s, so a D or a small E is enough impulse. Off a 1 m rail, 15 m/s needs an average acceleration near 15^2 / (2 * 1) = 112 m/s^2 before gravity is even added, which points at a high-thrust D or E rather than a long-burn. Looking those up.
