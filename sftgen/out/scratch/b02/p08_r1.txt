1. Understand: Apogee at least 250 m, and higher is better if it still fits the other rules (minimum, with a preference to exceed it). Total impulse no more than 20 N*s (maximum). Rail is 1 m (fixed). Landing speed is not stated, so the usual 6 m/s maximum applies. Wind not stated: 2 m/s. Vertical, sea level, single stage.

2. Feasibility: 20 N*s is a D, not an E. Drag-free speed for 250 m is sqrt(2 * 9.81 * 250) = 70 m/s, so the impulse can clear the floor if the rocket is light. The 1 m rod is the hard part: average acceleration has to be high or the exit speed falls under 15 m/s. I want the D closest to 20 N*s that still has a sharp thrust curve.
