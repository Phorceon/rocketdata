1. Understand: Two-stage, motors fixed. Booster is a Cesaroni O3400. Sustainer is a Cesaroni M2045. Apogee about 8,000 m, within 5%. Both sections land under 5 m/s (maximum). Wind about 2 m/s, so I will fly at 2 m/s. Rail and site are not stated: 1.5 m vertical rail, sea level.

An O plus an M may also bump into the 40,960 N*s total-impulse ceiling. I need both motors' impulse, thrust, diameter and mass before I size anything.
