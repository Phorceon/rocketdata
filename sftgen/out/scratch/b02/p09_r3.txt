The fins are the cause of the first two: the CP is not far enough back, and at Mach 3 the small planform is not spending the impulse. One change: sustainer span to 0.20 m and booster span to 0.28 m, with the chords grown to match. Chutes stay for this pass so I can see the drag effect on its own.
