1. Understand: Motor is fixed, a K1200. Altitude should be as high as is sensible, with no numeric floor. Landing under 15 ft/s. 15 * 0.3048 = 4.57 m/s, and "under" means the landing speed has to come in below that, not sit on it. Airframe under 110 mm across, so outer diameter stays below 0.110 m. Rail is 2.5 m, so that goes in every simulation. Wind is not stated: 2 m/s, vertical, sea level. L2 is context for the motor class, not a separate constraint.

2. Feasibility: A K motor in a minimum-diameter tube under 110 mm is the altitude path. The landing limit is tight for a heavy K rocket, so the chute has to be large. Looking up the K1200 before sizing the tube.
