1. Understand: Apogee at least 3,500 m. Wind is 4 m/s, and the landing has to stay inside 2,000 m of the pad, so drift is capped at 2,000 m. Dual deploy is required: a drogue at apogee and a main at a set altitude. Rail, angle and site are not stated: 1.5 m vertical rail, sea level. Landing speed is not stated, so the 6 m/s gate applies to the main.

2. Feasibility: A single big chute at apogee from 3,500 m at under 6 m/s would be in the air for something like 3,500 / 6 = 580 s, and 4 m/s of wind over that long a descent walks off the 2,000 m pad limit. Dual deploy is the way through that: fast under a drogue, then a main low enough that the slow part is short. The motor has to be a K or larger to clear 3,500 m in a tube that can hold two chutes.
