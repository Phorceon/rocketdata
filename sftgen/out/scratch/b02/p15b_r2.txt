2. Feasibility: HP-L1000W is an AeroTech L, 2702 N*s, 991 N average, burn 2.72 s, diameter 0.054 m, length 0.635 m, mass 2.19 kg. The only delay is 18 s, which is one ejection, not dual deploy. The motor flies plugged. An altimeter opens the drogue at apogee and the main at 250 m. The motor fits a 58 mm tube: mount OD 0.055 m leaves an inside diameter of 0.054 m for the motor, and the tube ID at 0.058 m with a 0.001 m wall is 0.056 m, so the mount fits.

3. Size: Liftoff guess 3.4 kg with the motor, a 1.20 m tube and two chutes. Thrust-to-weight is 991 / (3.4 * 9.81) = 29.7. Weight is 3.4 * 9.81 = 33.4 N, rail acceleration (991 - 33.4) / 3.4 = 282 m/s^2, exit speed sqrt(2 * 282 * 1.5) = 29.1 m/s. Drogue for about 2 kg at 18 m/s: area 2 * 2 * 9.81 / (1.225 * 0.8 * 324) = 0.124 m^2, diameter sqrt(4 * 0.124 / pi) = 0.40 m. I will fly 0.50 m. Main for about 2 kg at 5 m/s: area 2 * 2 * 9.81 / (1.225 * 0.8 * 25) = 1.60 m^2, diameter sqrt(4 * 1.60 / pi) = 1.43 m. I will fly 1.7 m so the landing stays under 6 m/s. The fast drogue is what keeps the drift inside 2,000 m at 4 m/s.
