1. Understand: Apogee at least 10,000 ft. 10,000 * 0.3048 = 3,048 m, a floor. The flight has to be back on the ground within 5 minutes of liftoff, so total flight time is at most 300 s. Dual deployment. Wind is around 12 mph. 12 * 0.447 = 5.36 m/s, and that wind goes in every simulation. Rail and site are not stated: 1.5 m vertical rail, sea level. Landing speed is not stated, so the 6 m/s gate applies to the main.

2. Feasibility: 300 s is the tight constraint. Ascent to 3,048 m is on the order of 20 s, which leaves about 280 s to come down. A single chute at under 6 m/s would take 3,048 / 6 = 508 s and would miss the clock. Dual deploy with a fast drogue is required. The motor has to clear 3,048 m without a huge coast that eats the clock. Looking up a K that can do that in a short dual-deploy airframe.
