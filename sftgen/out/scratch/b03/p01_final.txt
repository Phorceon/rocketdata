I300T-14 on a 44 mm phenolic airframe, birch-plywood fins, 0.91 m chute. It is stable and lands slowly, but it still misses the altitude.
Last flight at 3 m/s wind on the 1 m rod: 1,699.7 m apogee, 1.67 cal, 36.9 m/s off the rail, 3.8 m/s landing. That is 50 m under the 1,750 m minimum.
Fin drag near Mach 1.4 ate the spare altitude, and a third fin revision did not bring it back.
