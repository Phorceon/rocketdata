1. Understand: apogee at least 1,750 m (minimum). Landing at most 6 m/s (maximum). Wind is 3 m/s (fixed). The pad rod is 1 m (fixed), shorter than the usual 1.5 m rail, so the rocket has less distance to reach a safe exit speed. Launch angle and site altitude are not stated, so I assume 0 degrees and sea level. Nothing asks for a second stage, so this is a single-stage flight.
2. Feasibility: with no drag, the burnout speed for 1,750 m is sqrt(2*9.81*1750) = 185 m/s. A real 38 mm rocket keeps only part of that, so the motor has to be well into I class, and the 1 m rod means the average thrust has to be high or the rocket leaves the rail too slowly. I will look up a few H, I and J motors before choosing.
