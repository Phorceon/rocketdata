6. Uncertainty: at Mach 1.4 the OpenRocket drag model is the whole story, and the tool already warns that supersonic body drag is approximate. A real plywood fin at that speed may flutter, which would add drag the model does not include. Build mass will not match 0.705 kg exactly. Because the coast is drag-limited, a slightly heavier rocket may not lose much altitude, and a slightly sleeker nose may not gain the missing 50.3 m either. The next real step would be a larger motor, which I did not fly.
