AeroTech I300T-14 in a 54 mm phenolic airframe, 0.81 m long, with a 1.2 m chute. It fits the 110 mm envelope.
The flight reaches 1,442.9 m, leaves the 1.5 m rail at 40.9 m/s, and lands at 3.2 m/s, under the 4.5 m/s cap. Margin is 1.17 cal.
The 14 s delay opens the chute at 19.8 m/s, after apogee. Touchdown itself is slow, but that opening is hard on the canopy.
