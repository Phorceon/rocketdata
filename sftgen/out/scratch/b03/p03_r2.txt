2. Feasibility: the lookup returns the AeroTech I300T, class I, 436.8 N*s, average thrust 295.9 N, burn 1.47 s. The case is 0.038 m by 0.25 m and 0.4405 kg loaded, with delays of 6, 10 and 14 s. That is a legal L1 motor. Even with a heavy 54 mm airframe the bonus 250 m is easy; the 4.5 m/s landing is the number that needs a large chute. A 54 mm tube is well inside the 110 mm envelope and is a sensible L1 size, rather than a 38 mm dart.
3. Size: guessed liftoff mass 0.85 kg. Thrust-to-weight = 295.9 / (0.85 * 9.81) = 35.5. Pad weight is 0.85 * 9.81 = 8.34 N, rail acceleration (295.9 - 8.34) / 0.85 = 338 m/s^2, exit speed sqrt(2 * 338 * 1.5) = 31.8 m/s on the 1.5 m rail. Propellant is 0.222 kg, so descent mass is 0.85 - 0.222 = 0.63 kg. For 4 m/s under a Cd 0.8 chute, area = 2 * 0.63 * 9.81 / (1.225 * 0.8 * 4^2) = 0.788 m^2 and diameter = sqrt(4 * 0.788 / 3.1416) = 1.00 m. I will use 1.20 m so a heavier build still lands under 4.5 m/s. The 14 s delay plus the 1.47 s burn puts ejection near 16 s, which suits a high flight.
