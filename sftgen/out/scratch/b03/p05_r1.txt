1. Understand: apogee at least 500 m (minimum). Drift at most 400 m from the pad (maximum), for the whole rocket. Wind is 3 m/s (fixed). Recovery is dual deploy (fixed): a drogue at apogee and a main opening low. Landing speed is not given a number, so I keep the usual 6 m/s maximum on the main. Rail, angle and site are not given, so 1.5 m, vertical, sea level. Single stage.
2. Feasibility: 500 m is a modest altitude, and the small field is handled by not hanging under a big chute from apogee. A rough time budget is 400 / 3 = 133 s of flight before a 3 m/s wind walks the rocket 400 m, so a fast drogue and a low main fit easily. No-drag speed for 500 m is sqrt(2*9.81*500) = 99 m/s, which an E or a small F can provide. I need case size and impulse before I pick one.
