2. Feasibility: the lookup returns the AeroTech M1297W, class M, 5,413.8 N*s at 1,275 N average, burn 4.24 s. The case is 75 mm (0.075 m by 0.665 m) and 4.637 kg loaded, with 2.722 kg of propellant. A tube just outside the 75 mm case is the way to the highest flight; a fat airframe would give the altitude away.
3. Size: guessed liftoff mass 6.0 kg, mostly the motor. Thrust-to-weight = 1275 / (6.0 * 9.81) = 21.7. For 5 m/s at Cd 0.8, area = 2 * 3.28 * 9.81 / (1.225 * 0.8 * 5^2) = 2.63 m^2, diameter sqrt(4 * 2.63 / 3.1416) = 1.83 m. I will use a 2.0 m chute. The 4.6 kg motor will sit in the tail, so the fins have to be large or the margin will not clear 1 cal.
