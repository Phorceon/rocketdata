2. Feasibility: the database lists it as the M1297W, class M, 5,413.8 N*s, average thrust 1,275 N, burn 4.24 s. Case 0.075 m by 0.665 m, loaded mass 4.637 kg, propellant 2.722 kg. The delay list is only 0 s, so there is no ejection charge and the chute must open on an altimeter at apogee. A 90 mm tube is only a little larger than the 75 mm case, which is the simple way to keep the drag down.
3. Size: I am guessing 6.0 kg at liftoff. Thrust-to-weight = 1275 / (6.0 * 9.81) = 21.7. Weight is 6.0 * 9.81 = 58.9 N, so the rail acceleration is (1275 - 58.9) / 6.0 = 203 m/s^2 and the 3.05 m rail gives sqrt(2 * 203 * 3.05) = 35.2 m/s. After the propellant is gone the descent mass is about 6.0 - 2.72 = 3.28 kg. A 5 m/s landing at Cd 0.8 needs 2 * 3.28 * 9.81 / (1.225 * 0.8 * 5^2) = 2.63 m^2, which is a diameter of sqrt(4 * 2.63 / 3.1416) = 1.83 m. I will round up to a 2.0 m canopy. Four plywood fins, fairly large, because 4.6 kg of motor in the tail will otherwise leave the CP too far forward.
4. Draft: fiberglass ogive nose 0.25 m long with a 3 mm wall, then a 1.20 m phenolic tube, 0.090 m outside diameter, 2 mm wall. Motor mount 0.080 m outside and 0.70 m long, M1297W plugged. Four fins, root 0.32 m, tip 0.12 m, span 0.20 m, sweep 0.16 m, 6 mm birch plywood. Chute 2.0 m at apogee. Flying at 4.47 m/s wind on the 3.05 m rail.
