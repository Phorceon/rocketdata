Estes F50-6 in a 41 mm cardboard rocket with 0.028 kg of nose clay and one 0.55 m chute.
In 6 m/s wind it reaches 716.7 m, about 2% over 700 m, leaves the rail at 35 m/s, and lands at 3.4 m/s. Margin is 1.25 cal.
The 6 s delay opens the chute at 16.3 m/s, slightly before apogee. Touchdown itself is well under 6 m/s.
