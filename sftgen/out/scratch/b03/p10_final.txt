Estes E12-6 in a 33 mm cardboard rocket, with 5 g of nose clay and a 0.45 m chute. A D12 on a wider tube did not reach 400 m, so this is the E.
On the 2 m rail it reaches 526.4 m, holds 1.38 cal, and lands at 3.0 m/s, under the 5.5 m/s cap.
The clay is what moved the margin over 1 cal. Leave it out and the rocket sits just under that floor.
