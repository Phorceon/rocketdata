Infeasible: one I205 cannot reach 65,000 ft in a single stage. Drag, not the safety gates, is what stops it.
The lightest stable build, a plugged HP-I205W in a 38 mm cardboard tube, reaches 1,664.1 m with 1.3 cal and a 4.2 m/s landing.
That is about 12 times short of 19,800 m. A larger motor, or more than one stage, is what 65,000 ft actually takes.
