AeroTech G64W-10 in a 54 mm cardboard rocket with large balsa fins and an 0.85 m chute. The motor is under an I, so it is legal on an L1.
Apogee is 732.9 m, about 5% over 700 m and inside the band. Landing is 3.0 m/s, under 4.57 m/s. Margin is 1.24 cal.
The 10 s delay opens the chute at 20 m/s, after apogee. Touchdown is slow; the opening is the rough part.
