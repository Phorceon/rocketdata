2. Feasibility: the E12 is only 27.1 N*s at 11.2 N, far short of 610 m. The G64W is 118.0 N*s and 0.1512 kg, and on a 54 mm rocket it has already flown well above 700 m, so it is more motor than this floor needs. The Estes F50 is 76.8 N*s at 55.8 N average, a 29 mm case, 0.098 m long, 0.0836 kg, with only a 6 s delay. That impulse should clear 610 m in a slim tube, and the case fits inside 0.0508 m.
3. Size: guessed liftoff mass 0.22 kg. Thrust-to-weight = 55.8 / (0.22 * 9.81) = 25.9. Pad weight 0.22 * 9.81 = 2.16 N, rail acceleration (55.8 - 2.16) / 0.22 = 244 m/s^2, exit speed sqrt(2 * 244 * 1.5) = 27.1 m/s. Propellant is 0.038 kg, descent mass 0.22 - 0.038 = 0.182 kg. Aiming at 4.5 m/s, under the 5.5 m/s cap, area = 2 * 0.182 * 9.81 / (1.225 * 0.8 * 4.5^2) = 0.180 m^2, diameter sqrt(4 * 0.180 / 3.1416) = 0.479 m. I will use 0.55 m. Burn is 1.37 s, so the 6 s delay ejects near 7.4 s.
