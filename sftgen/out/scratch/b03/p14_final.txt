Estes C6-5 in a 33 mm cardboard rocket with an 18 mm mount, balsa fins, 8 g of nose clay and a 0.30 m chute, launched from a 1 m rod.
Apogee is 255.8 m, about 2% over 250 m and inside the band. Landing is 3.8 m/s, under 6 m/s. Margin is 1.34 cal. Rod exit is 19.7 m/s.
The 5 s delay opens the chute just after apogee at 2.8 m/s.
