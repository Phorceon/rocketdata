2. Feasibility: the B6 is 4.3 N*s and the only delay is 0 s, so it is both short on impulse and plugged. The Estes C6 is 8.8 N*s at 4.8 N average, 18 mm, 0.07 m long, 0.0231 kg, with 3, 5 and 7 s delays. It fits the 18 mm mount. Average thrust is only 4.8 N, so on a 1 m rod the rocket has to stay near 40 g or the exit speed drops under 15 m/s. I will use the 5 s delay.
3. Size: guessed liftoff mass 0.038 kg. Thrust-to-weight = 4.8 / (0.038 * 9.81) = 12.9. Pad weight 0.038 * 9.81 = 0.373 N, rail acceleration (4.8 - 0.373) / 0.038 = 117 m/s^2, exit speed on the 1 m rod sqrt(2 * 117 * 1) = 15.3 m/s. Propellant is 0.011 kg, descent mass 0.038 - 0.011 = 0.027 kg. At 5 m/s, under 6 m/s, area = 2 * 0.027 * 9.81 / (1.225 * 0.8 * 5^2) = 0.0216 m^2, diameter sqrt(4 * 0.0216 / 3.1416) = 0.166 m. I will use 0.30 m so the landing stays soft even if the rocket is a little heavier. Burn is 1.83 s, so the 5 s delay ejects near 6.8 s.
