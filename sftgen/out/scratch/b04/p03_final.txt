Single-stage 81 mm fiberglass rocket on a plugged AeroTech L1520T, with a 2.2 m apogee chute and 0.20 kg of nose weight.
The simulator gives 3594 m, above the 2740 m floor, 1.25 cal of margin, and a 4.7 m/s landing.
It is supersonic (Mach 1.66), and the tool warns that drag at that speed is approximate.
