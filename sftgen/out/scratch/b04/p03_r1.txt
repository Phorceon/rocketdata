1. Understand: motor is fixed, an AeroTech L1520. Goal is maximum altitude, with a floor of at least 9,000 ft. 9000 * 0.3048 = 2740 m. Landing speed is a maximum of 5 m/s. Single stage (fixed). Stable means a launch margin of at least 1 cal and a positive margin through the burn. Wind, rail and site are not stated, so I use 2 m/s, a 1.5 m rail, vertical, sea level.
2. Feasibility: 2740 m with no drag needs sqrt(2 * 9.81 * 2740) = 232 m/s, which an L motor clears easily. The real job is keeping the rocket light and narrow so it goes as high as it can, then still landing under 5 m/s. I need the L1520's diameter, impulse and delays before I size the airframe.
