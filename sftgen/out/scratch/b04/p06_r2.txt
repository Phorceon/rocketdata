2. Feasibility: G54 resolves to the AeroTech G54W, class G, 81.1 N*s, 56.3 N average, burn 1.44 s, 29 mm. That is under the high-power line. Delays are 6, 10 and 14 s. 81 N*s will not make a heavy rocket climb, so the sensible altitude build is a light 41 mm cardboard tube, stable but not over-finned, because 4.47 m/s of wind will weathercock a very stable rocket.
3. Size: guessed liftoff mass 0.25 kg. Thrust-to-weight = 56.3 / (0.25 * 9.81) = 23.0. Rail acceleration (56.3 - 2.45) / 0.25 = 215 m/s^2, exit speed sqrt(2 * 215 * 1.5) = 25.4 m/s. Descent mass 0.25 - 0.046 = 0.204 kg. At 5 m/s and Cd 0.8, area = 2 * 0.204 * 9.81 / (1.225 * 0.8 * 5^2) = 0.163 m^2, diameter sqrt(4 * 0.163 / 3.1416) = 0.456 m. A 0.55 m chute stays under 6 m/s without hanging in the wind forever. Burnout at 1.44 s and a coast of roughly 8 s fits the 10 s delay better than the 6 s one.
4. Draft: 41 mm cardboard, 0.48 m long, polystyrene ogive, three balsa fins of modest span, 29 mm mount, G54W-10, 0.55 m chute on ejection. Wind 4.47 m/s, rail 1.5 m.
