4. Check: the simulator gives a static margin of -0.07 cal at rail exit, and -0.07 cal through the burn. That fails both the 1 cal launch gate and the requirement to stay positive during the burn. The rocket is unstable, so the 808.2 m apogee is not a flight I can keep. Landing 3.8 m/s is under 6, rail exit 30.4 m/s and thrust-to-weight 26.5 would have passed. Liftoff mass is 0.217 kg, a bit under my 0.25 kg guess, which put the CG even farther back on the G54W. The modest fins were too small.
5. Fix: -0.07 cal is the failing value. Fin area was (0.08 + 0.03) / 2 * 0.055 = 0.0030 m^2. New fins are root 0.13 m, tip 0.045 m, span 0.10 m, sweep 0.06 m, area (0.13 + 0.045) / 2 * 0.10 = 0.0088 m^2, and 0.0088 / 0.0030 = 2.9 times the area. That is a normal sport fin for a 41 mm tube, large enough to get the CP behind the motor without turning it into a weathercock. Tube, nose, motor and chute stay the same.
