1. Understand: motor class is a maximum of G, because the flyer is not certified. Apogee asked is 30,000 ft. 30000 * 0.3048 = 9144 m, and the wording is a target to reach, so I treat it as a minimum. Landing limit is not stated, so 6 m/s. Single stage assumed. Rail 1.5 m, wind 2 m/s, vertical, sea level.
2. Feasibility: 9144 m with no drag needs sqrt(2 * 9.81 * 9144) = 424 m/s. A G motor tops out near 160 N*s. On a very light rocket that impulse can produce that speed only if drag is ignored, and a real 29 mm airframe at a few hundred metres per second will not. I want the largest G in the catalog before I decide it cannot be done.
