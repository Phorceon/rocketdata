2. Feasibility: G80T is class G, 135.6 N*s, 80.6 N, burn 1.68 s, 29 mm, loaded mass only 0.129 kg. G80T is the best legal motor. Even then, drag on a 29 mm nose at a few hundred metres per second will cap the coast far below 9144 m. I will fly the lightest stable airframe and see how short it falls.
