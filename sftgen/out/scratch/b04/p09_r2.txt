2. Feasibility: the D12 is 16.8 N*s, 10.4 N average, 24 mm, delays to 7 s, class D, under the H cap. The C6 is only 4.8 N. With a 0.060 kg egg the liftoff mass is near 0.20 kg, and 4.8 / (0.20 * 9.81) = 2.45, under the 3:1 gate, so the C6 cannot lift this. The D12 can. A long flight from 175 m is a chute problem: 175 / 50 = 3.5 m/s average sink if the coast is a few seconds and the rest of a 57 s flight is descent.
3. Size: thrust-to-weight on the D12 is 10.4 / (0.20 * 9.81) = 5.30. Rail acceleration (10.4 - 1.96) / 0.20 = 42.2 m/s^2, exit speed sqrt(2 * 42.2 * 1.5) = 11.2 m/s on average thrust. The D12 spikes to 29.7 N, so the real exit should clear 15 m/s. Descent mass 0.20 - 0.021 = 0.179 kg. At 3.4 m/s and Cd 0.8, area = 2 * 0.179 * 9.81 / (1.225 * 0.8 * 3.4^2) = 0.310 m^2, diameter sqrt(4 * 0.310 / 3.1416) = 0.628 m. I will fit a 0.65 m chute. Burn is 1.61 s, so the 7 s delay ejects near 9 s.
