Two-stage fiberglass rocket: Cesaroni 21062O3400-P booster and AeroTech M1297W sustainer, both plugged, with a 5.2 m booster chute and a 4.0 m sustainer chute.
The simulator gives 8392.7 m on a 2.5 m rail. The booster lands at 4.1 m/s. The sustainer is still descending at 4.0 m/s when the run hits its time limit.
Launch margin is 3.26 cal and rail exit is 23.3 m/s.
