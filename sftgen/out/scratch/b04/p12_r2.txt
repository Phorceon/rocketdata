2. Feasibility: 21062O3400-P is class O, 21,041 N*s, 3,421 N average, 98 mm, 16.842 kg, and the only delay is 0, so it flies plugged. M1297W is class M, 5,413.8 N*s, 1,275 N, 75 mm, 4.637 kg, also delay 0. 21041 + 5413.8 = 26,455 N*s, under the 40,960 N*s cap. The booster separates at burnout and the sustainer lights then. Both chutes open at apogee.
3. Size: motors alone are 16.842 + 4.637 = 21.5 kg, and a fiberglass airframe brings liftoff to about 30 kg. 3421 / (30 * 9.81) = 11.6, above 3. On the 2.5 m rail, (3421 - 30 * 9.81) / 30 = 104 m/s^2 and sqrt(2 * 104 * 2.5) = 23 m/s. Both bodies are 0.120 m so the diameters match. The sustainer chute has to land about 9 kg under 4.57 m/s: 2 * 9 * 9.81 / (1.225 * 0.8 * 4.2^2) = 10.2 m^2, and sqrt(4 * 10.2 / 3.14) = 3.6 m, so a 4.0 m chute. The booster gets the same. Booster fins are large because the O sits in the tail.
4. Draft: 21062O3400-P booster, M1297W sustainer, 4.0 m chutes, rail 2.5 m.
