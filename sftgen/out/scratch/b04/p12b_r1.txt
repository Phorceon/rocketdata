1. Understand: two stages. The booster motor class is O and the sustainer class is M, both fixed. Apogee is a minimum of 7,500 m. Every section lands under 15 ft/s, which is 15 * 0.3048 = 4.57 m/s. The rail is 2.5 m and has to be passed on the flight. Wind and site are not stated, so 2 m/s and sea level, launched vertical. Both stages need their own recovery. Total impulse has to stay at or under 40,960 N*s, so a small O and a small M are safer than the top of those classes.
2. Feasibility: 7,500 m with no drag needs sqrt(2 * 9.81 * 7500) = 384 m/s. An O starts just over 20,000 N*s and an M just over 5,000 N*s, so the impulse is there if the airframe stays reasonable. Looking up a small O and a small M.
