2. Feasibility: 21062O3400-P is class O, 21,041 N*s, 3,421 N average, 98 mm and 16.842 kg. The only delay is 0, so it flies plugged. M1297W is class M, 5,413.8 N*s, 1,275 N, 75 mm and 4.637 kg, also delay 0. 21041 + 5413.8 = 26,500 N*s, under the 40,960 N*s cap. The booster separates at burnout and the sustainer lights then. Both chutes open at apogee.
3. Size: the motors are 16.842 + 4.637 = 21.5 kg, and fiberglass around them brings liftoff to about 35 kg. 3421 / (35 * 9.81) = 10.0. On the 2.5 m rail, (3421 - 35 * 9.81) / 35 = 88 m/s^2 and sqrt(2 * 88 * 2.5) = 21 m/s. Both bodies are 0.120 m so the diameters match. The booster comes down near 15 kg: 2 * 15 * 9.81 / (1.225 * 0.8 * 4.2^2) = 17.0 m^2, and sqrt(4 * 17.0 / 3.14) = 4.7 m, so a 5.0 m chute. The sustainer is near 8 kg: 2 * 8 * 9.81 / (1.225 * 0.8 * 4.2^2) = 9.1 m^2, and sqrt(4 * 9.1 / 3.14) = 3.4 m, so a 3.6 m chute. Booster fin span 0.28 m is 0.28 / 0.120 = 2.3 calibers, which the heavy tail needs.
4. Draft: 21062O3400-P booster, M1297W sustainer, 5.0 m and 3.6 m chutes, rail 2.5 m.
