3. Size: guessed liftoff mass 0.045 kg. Thrust-to-weight = 4.8 / (0.045 * 9.81) = 10.9. On the 1 m rod, average acceleration is (4.8 - 0.045 * 9.81) / 0.045 = 97 m/s^2, so sqrt(2 * 97 * 1) = 13.9 m/s using average thrust. That is under 15 m/s, and only the 14.1 N spike can save it. Descent mass is about 0.045 - 0.011 = 0.034 kg. For a 5 m/s landing at Cd 0.8 the area is 2 * 0.034 * 9.81 / (1.225 * 0.8 * 5^2) = 0.027 m^2, diameter sqrt(4 * 0.027 / 3.1416) = 0.19 m. I'll fit a 0.20 m chute.
