1. Understand: two stages, fixed motors. Booster is a Cesaroni O3400. Sustainer is a Cesaroni M2045. Apogee is a minimum of 7,500 m, not an "about". Every piece that separates must land at or under 6 m/s, so both the booster and the sustainer need recovery. Rail, wind, and launch angle are not stated, so I assume a 1.5 m rail, 2 m/s wind, and a vertical sea-level launch.
2. Feasibility: 7,500 m with no drag needs sqrt(2 * 9.81 * 7500) = 384 m/s at burnout of the upper stage. An O plus an M has a lot of impulse, so the altitude is plausible if the rocket is not huge. Both stages still have to come down under 6 m/s, which means large chutes. I need the motor diameters, masses, and thrust before I draw the airframe.
