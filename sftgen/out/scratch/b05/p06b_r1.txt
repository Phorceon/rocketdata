1. Understand: two stages with the motors fixed. Booster is a Cesaroni O3400. Sustainer is a Cesaroni M2045. Apogee is a minimum of 7,500 m. Each piece that separates has to land at or under 6 m/s, so both stages need their own chute. Rail and wind are not given, so I assume a 1.5 m rail and 2 m/s wind. Vertical, sea level.
2. Feasibility: a drag-free coast to 7,500 m needs sqrt(2 * 9.81 * 7500) = 384 m/s after the upper burn. An O under an M should have the impulse if the airframe is not absurdly heavy. Both motors are large, so the chutes will be large too. I need diameters, masses, and thrust before drawing tubes.
