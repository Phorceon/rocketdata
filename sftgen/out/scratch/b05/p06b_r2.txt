3. Size: the sustainer flies alone with the M in its tail, so its fins have to be large or it tumbles at ignition. Those fins also sit forward on the stack and pull the combined CP forward, so the booster fins have to be larger still or the launch margin goes negative. Sustainer descent mass is about 8 kg. At 5 m/s and Cd 0.8 the area is 2 * 8 * 9.81 / (1.225 * 0.8 * 5^2) = 6.4 m^2, diameter sqrt(4 * 6.4 / 3.1416) = 2.9 m. I'll fit 2.8 m. Booster descent mass is about 12 kg, area 2 * 12 * 9.81 / (1.225 * 0.8 * 5^2) = 9.6 m^2, diameter sqrt(4 * 9.6 / 3.1416) = 3.5 m. I'll fit 3.8 m so the heavier piece has margin under 6 m/s.
