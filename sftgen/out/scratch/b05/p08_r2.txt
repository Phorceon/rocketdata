2. Feasibility: L2200G is 5104 N*s and M1297W is 5414 N*s, both 75 mm. That is light for 8,500 m once the airframe is real. N2000W is 13263 N*s at 1927 N, 98 mm, 12.3 kg loaded, burn 6.87 s. N3800-LW is similar impulse with more thrust. O3400 is 21041 N*s, about 21041 / 13263 = 1.6 times the N2000, and likely sails past the top of the band. I'll use the N2000W. It has no ejection delay, so dual deploy is electronic and the motor stays plugged.
3. Size: guessed liftoff mass 19 kg. Thrust-to-weight is 1930 / (19 * 9.81) = 10. On the 2.4 m rail, acceleration is (1930 - 19 * 9.81) / 19 = 92 m/s^2 and rail exit is sqrt(2 * 92 * 2.4) = 21 m/s. Propellant is about 7.7 kg, so descent mass is 19 - 7.7 = 11 kg. Main at 5 m/s needs 2 * 11 * 9.81 / (1.225 * 0.8 * 5^2) = 8.8 m^2, diameter sqrt(4 * 8.8 / 3.1416) = 3.3 m. I'll fit a 3.4 m main opening at 400 m, and a 0.70 m drogue at apogee so the long fall in 4.5 m/s wind stays well inside 6,400 m.
4. Draft: 112 mm fiberglass, N2000W in a 98 mm mount, four plywood fins, drogue plus main. Wind 4.5 m/s and rail 2.4 m go in the call.
