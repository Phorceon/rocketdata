2. Feasibility: Estes C6 is only 8.8 N*s at 4.8 N, and the C11 is the same 8.8 N*s in a heavier 24 mm case. AeroTech C18W is 9.8 N*s at 19.3 N, 18 mm, 0.081 m long, 0.021 kg, and it has no 0 s delay. Three of them are 3 * 9.8 = 29 N*s. That is still a small budget for 650 m, so the airframe stays 22 mm and short, and every motor is plugged so separation is at burnout rather than a delay charge. The C18W thrust clears a 1.5 m rail that a C6 stack would not.
3. Size: guessed stack mass 0.11 kg. Booster thrust-to-weight is 19.3 / (0.11 * 9.81) = 18. Each spent stage is about 0.04 kg. A 5 m/s landing, under the 6 m/s limit, needs 2 * 0.04 * 9.81 / (1.225 * 0.8 * 5^2) = 0.032 m^2, diameter sqrt(4 * 0.032 / 3.1416) = 0.20 m. I'll fit a 0.22 m chute on every stage.
4. Draft: sustainer 0.25 m with the nose and small fins, a 0.14 m middle stage, and a 0.16 m booster with larger fins so the stack margin stays positive. All three motors are C18W, upper stages light at burnout, lower stages separate at burnout. Defaults: 1.5 m rail, 2 m/s wind.
