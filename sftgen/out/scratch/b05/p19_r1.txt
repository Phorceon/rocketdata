1. Understand: apogee is about 2,400 m, so the band is 2400 * 0.95 = 2280 m to 2400 * 1.05 = 2520 m. Total flight time has to be under 200 s. Dual deploy is required: a drogue at apogee and a main lower down. Landing speed is not stated, so I assume 6 m/s on the main. Rail and wind are not stated. I assume a 1.5 m rail, 2 m/s wind, vertical, sea level, one stage.
2. Feasibility: 2,400 m with no drag is sqrt(2 * 9.81 * 2400) = 217 m/s. A single chute at 5 m/s from that height takes 2400 / 5 = 480 s, which blows the 200 s window, so the drogue has to do most of the descent. A K motor on a 54 mm rocket is the class to check.
