1. Understand: apogee is at least 600 m. Total flight has to be under 3 minutes, and 3 * 60 = 180 s. One main chute, opening at apogee. Landing speed is not stated, so I assume 6 m/s. Rail and wind are not stated. I assume a 1.5 m rail, 2 m/s wind, vertical, sea level, one stage. The section has to reach the ground.
2. Feasibility: 600 m with no drag is sqrt(2 * 9.81 * 600) = 108 m/s. A single chute at 5 m/s from 600 m takes about 600 / 5 = 120 s, inside 180 s. If the rocket goes much past about 6 * 170 = 1,000 m, a 6 m/s landing cannot finish in time. An F or a small G on a 41 mm rocket is the range. Looking those up.
