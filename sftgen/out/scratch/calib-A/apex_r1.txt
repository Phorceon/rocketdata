1. Understand: apogee about 9,000 ft = 9000 * 0.3048 = 2743 m (approximate, aim within 5%). Drift at most 1,500 m from the pad in 8 mph = 8 * 0.44704 = 3.6 m/s wind (maximum, the hard constraint). Touchdown limit not stated, so I assume 6 m/s. Rail not stated, so 1.5 m. Dual deployment is permitted, and at this altitude it is nearly required.
2. Feasibility: why dual deploy matters here — drifting 1,500 m at 3.6 m/s allows 1500 / 3.6 = 417 s of total descent. A single chute landing at 5 m/s from 2,743 m would take 2743 / 5.5 = 499 s, too long. A drogue descent near 25 m/s down to a 300 m main cuts that to roughly a third. For altitude, ballistic speed to 2,743 m is sqrt(2*9.81*2743) = 232 m/s before drag, so this is J to K territory on a 5 to 8 kg airframe. Checking J420R, K535 and K740.
