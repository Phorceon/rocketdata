2. Feasibility: the J420R at 651.2 N*s is far too weak for 2,743 m on a dual-deploy build. The K740 returns 1854.6 N*s at 746.9 N average, 54 mm, 1.469 kg loaded, which is the right class; the K535W at 1428.6 N*s is the fallback if this overshoots low.
3. Size: airframe about 4 kg plus the motor puts liftoff near 5.5 kg. Thrust-to-weight = 746.9 / (5.5 * 9.81) = 13.8. Rail exit on 1.5 m: a = (746.9 - 54) / 5.5 = 126 m/s^2, so v = sqrt(2 * 126 * 1.5) = 19.4 m/s. Ideal delta-v = 1854.6 / 5.5 = 337 m/s before losses; on a 76 mm body that should top out around the 2,700 m region. Descent mass is about 5.5 - 0.9 = 4.6 kg. Drogue 0.5 m: v = sqrt(2 * 4.6 * 9.81 / (1.225 * 0.8 * 0.196)) = 21.7 m/s down to 300 m. Main 2.2 m at Cd 0.8 gives sqrt(2 * 4.6 * 9.81 / (1.225 * 0.8 * 3.8)) = 4.9 m/s. Drift budget: (2700 - 300) / 22 = 109 s under drogue plus 300 / 5 = 60 s under main, about 170 s total, times 3.6 m/s wind = 612 m of drift, well under 1,500 m.
