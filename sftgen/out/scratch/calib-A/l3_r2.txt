Feasibility: the M1520 returns 7579.1 N*s at 1557.6 N average over a 4.86 s burn, 98 mm diameter, 6.718 kg loaded, no ejection delays, so recovery must be altimeter-triggered, which matches the dual-deploy plan. A 98 mm motor needs at least a ~0.11 m airframe; on a 13 to 14 kg build the thrust-to-weight = 1557.6 / (14 * 9.81) = 11.3, and 2,000 m is trivially inside an M's reach.
3. Size: liftoff estimate about 14 kg (airframe ~7 kg plus the 6.72 kg motor). Rail exit on the 3 m rail: a = (1557.6 - 137) / 14 = 101 m/s^2, giving v = sqrt(2 * 101 * 3) = 24.6 m/s. Ideal delta-v = 7579 / 14 = 541 m/s, less about 48 m/s of gravity loss over the burn; the ballistic ceiling is far above 2,000 m so drag at 0.11 m diameter decides where it actually tops out, probably 3 to 5 km. Descent mass is about 14 - 3.74 = 10.3 kg. A main giving 5 m/s at Cd 0.8 needs A = 2 * 10 * 9.81 / (1.225 * 0.8 * 25) = 8.0 m^2, a 3.2 m canopy. Drogue at apogee: a 0.7 m chute descends at about sqrt(2 * 10 * 9.81 / (1.225 * 0.8 * 0.385)) = 22.8 m/s, fast but stable for the fall to 300 m.
