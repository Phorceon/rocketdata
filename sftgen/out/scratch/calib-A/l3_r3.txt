4. Check: the simulated apogee of 4381.5 m clears the 2,000 m floor by a wide margin. Rail exit is 27.6 m/s off the 3 m rail, thrust-to-weight 12.7, margin 1.07 cal at rail exit and through the burn, touchdown 4.9 m/s under the 3.2 m main, drogue fired at apogee (26.0 s) and the main at 300 m. Two honest caveats in the output: the flight peaked at Mach 1.43 where the body drag model is least reliable, and the drogue opened at 21.8 m/s, which the simulator flags as a high-speed deployment.
5. Fix: none needed; every gate and every stated requirement passed on this draft.
6. Uncertainty: a 1.07 cal margin is legal but thin for a rocket this size; added finish weight or slightly bigger fins would widen it. At Mach 1.43 the transonic drag model carries real error, so the 4381.5 m apogee could move by several hundred metres either way. The drogue opened at 21.8 m/s of mostly horizontal speed; a windier day or more weathercocking makes that worse, which is the first thing an L3 flight review would question. Landing speed assumes the Cd 0.8 canopy model and a tight pack into the 0.8 m bay.
