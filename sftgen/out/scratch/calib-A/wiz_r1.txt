1. Understand: apogee above 175 m (minimum, not a target to hit exactly). Motor class at most D (fixed limit, low power only). Wind about 3 m/s. Rail length and landing speed are not given, so I assume the standard 1.5 m rail and a 6 m/s touchdown limit. Single stage keeps it simple.
2. Feasibility: 175 m is modest for low power. A ballistic pass needs v = sqrt(2*9.81*175) = 58.6 m/s at burnout, before drag. A C-class motor on a light airframe should clear it with room, and even a B might work, but since 175 m is a floor I want real impulse numbers for the candidates: B6, C6 and the D ceiling, D12.
