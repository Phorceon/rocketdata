Rail, wind, angle and site are not stated, so I assume a 1.5 m rail, 2 m/s wind, a vertical launch and sea level. One stage, since nothing asks for staging.
2. Feasibility: reaching 650 m with drag ignored and the whole impulse dumped at liftoff still needs sqrt(2 * 9.81 * 650) = 113 m/s upward. The coast from that speed back to zero takes 113 / 9.81 = 11.5 s, which is already over the 10 s cap, before a real burn, drag, or the descent. Altitude and flight time disagree with each other.
