2. Feasibility: the F15 is 49.6 N*s at 14.5 N, the G80T is 135.6 N*s at 80.6 N, and the H128W is 177.8 N*s at 152.6 N. The vacuum round trip to 650 m and back is 2 * 11.5 = 23.0 s, so the 10 s cap is impossible on any of them, and a 4.57 m/s landing makes the flight longer still. The nearest useful rocket is one that still aims at about 650 m and lands under 4.57 m/s. The F15 only makes 14.5 N, which is soft if the airframe has to be heavy enough to keep the apogee from running away, and 135.6 / 49.6 = 2.73, so a G80T has nearly three times the impulse. I am checking a hotter F-class motor before freezing the draft.
