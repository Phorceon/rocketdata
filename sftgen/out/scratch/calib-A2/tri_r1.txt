1. Understand: a three-motor cluster, and no motor above a C (maximum class, fixed). Apogee about 1,000 ft, and 1000 * 0.3048 = 305 m (approximate, within 5%). Landing speed at most 5.5 m/s. The pad rod is 3 ft, and 3 * 0.3048 = 0.914 m (fixed). Wind, angle and site are not stated, so I assume 2 m/s wind, a vertical launch and sea level. One stage.
2. Feasibility: 305 m with drag ignored needs a burnout speed of sqrt(2 * 9.81 * 305) = 77 m/s. A cluster body is wide, so drag will eat a good part of that and I want something like 20 to 30 N*s total, which points at three C motors rather than three B motors. The rod is only 0.914 m, so a low-thrust long burn may leave the rail too slowly. I am looking up the C6, the quicker C11, and a B6 in case three C motors fly too high.
