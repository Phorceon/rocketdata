4. Check: the simulator gives an apogee of 4095 m with a max velocity of 757.7 m/s (Mach 2.24), so the L1400 gets a lot out of a light minimum-diameter build. Every gate passes: rail exit 37.8 m/s (at least 15), thrust-to-weight 40.7 (at least 3), margin 3.15 cal at rail exit and 2.72 cal minimum during the burn (at least 1.0 and above 0), landing 5.3 m/s (under 6), deployment at 21.9 s right at apogee. Liftoff mass came back 3.56 kg, close to my 3.5 kg estimate. The only flag is a warning that body calculations may not be entirely accurate at supersonic speeds.
5. Fix: none needed — every check passed on the first draft.
6. Uncertainty: it reached Mach 2.24, where the drag model is approximate, so the 4095 m could be off by hundreds of metres either way. A margin of 3.15 cal is more than required and the rocket will weathercock; at that speed a real phenolic build also has to survive serious aero loads. Descent is 5.3 m/s under a 1.5 m chute, but from 4 km even 2 m/s wind gave 1390 m of drift, so it is a long walk on a windy day.
