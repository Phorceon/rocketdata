The C4 is no help (8.9 N*s is the same impulse as the C6), so the B7 it is. New sizing on the B7: keep the 41.6 mm tube for drag but back to 0.35 m of it, a 0.10 m solid balsa ogive, a 13 mm mount, and three balsa fins at the tail. Estimated liftoff mass about 0.055 kg. Thrust-to-weight = 7.2 / (0.055 * 9.81) = 13.3. Rail exit on average thrust alone: a = (7.2 - 0.055 * 9.81) / 0.055 = 121 m/s^2, so v = sqrt(2 * 121 * 1.5) = 19 m/s, comfortably over the gate. Descent at about 0.05 kg under a 0.25 m canopy: v = sqrt(2 * 0.05 * 9.81 / (1.225 * 0.8 * 0.049)) = 4.5 m/s. Apogee is the open question: half the impulse on roughly half the mass lands somewhere around 120 to 140 m. Burnout is at 0.69 s and apogee should come near 4.5 s, so the 4 s delay ejects at about 4.7 s, just past the top.
