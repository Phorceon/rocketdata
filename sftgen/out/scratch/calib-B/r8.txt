1. Understand: the motor is fixed, an L1400 (exact match required). The goal is the highest sensible altitude it can lift a stable single-stage rocket to (open goal). Landing must stay under 6 m/s (maximum). "Stable" means a static margin of at least 1.0 cal, and I will aim near 1.5. One stage only. No wind or rail length is stated, so I assume the 1.5 m rail and 2 m/s wind defaults.
2. Feasibility: an L1400 is an L-class motor, so somewhere in the 5 to 10 kN*s impulse range and heavy — this is a high-power build with a big mount and a large recovery load. Getting the most altitude means the lightest, lowest-drag airframe that still holds the motor and keeps the margin, and a parachute sized for the real descent mass. Before sizing anything I need the motor's actual impulse, thrust, diameter and mass.
