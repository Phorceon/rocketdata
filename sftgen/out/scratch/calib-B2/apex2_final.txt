A 76 mm fiberglass rocket on a plugged Cesaroni 2765L730-P, with a 0.30 m drogue at apogee and a 1.5 m main at 150 m.
The simulator gives 3507.8 m and 177.0 s in a 2 m/s wind, landing at 5.2 m/s with 2.39 cal of margin.
That clears 2,500 m and the 240 s limit. It takes a Level 3 motor to do it in a rocket this size.
