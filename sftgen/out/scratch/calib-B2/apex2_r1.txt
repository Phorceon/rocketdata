1. Understand: the rocket has to reach at least 2,500 m and be on the ground in under 4 minutes. That time cap is 4 * 60 = 240 s. Dual deploy is allowed. Wind is about 2 m/s. Rail length, launch angle and landing speed are not given, so I am using a 1.5 m rail, a straight-up launch and a 6 m/s touchdown limit. One stage, built like a normal high-power kit rather than a minimum-diameter experiment.
2. Feasibility: a single parachute cannot hit both the landing limit and the clock. Coming down from 2,500 m at 6 m/s already takes 2500 / 6 = 417 s, before adding the climb, so the flight would blow past 240 s. The drogue has to do most of the descent and the main can only be out for the last few hundred metres. With no drag, burnout speed for 2,500 m is sqrt(2 * 9.81 * 2500) = 221 m/s, and drag will take a big cut, so I want something in the high-J to L range in a 76 mm tube.
