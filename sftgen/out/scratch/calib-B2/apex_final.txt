A 76 mm fiberglass airframe with a plugged Cesaroni 2765L730-P, a 0.30 m apogee drogue and a 1.5 m main at 150 m.
The simulator gives 3507.5 m and a 176.8 s flight in 2 m/s wind, landing at 5.1 m/s with 2.36 cal of margin.
That is over the 2,500 m minimum and under the 240 s limit. The motor is Level 3.
