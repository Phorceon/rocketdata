1. Understand: two stages, fixed. The booster motor has to be L class and the sustainer K class. Apogee at least 5,000 m. Each stage lands at 4.5 m/s or slower, which is tighter than the usual 6 m/s. Wind is about 20 km/h, and 20 / 3.6 = 5.6 m/s. The rail is 2.5 m. Launch angle and site altitude are not stated, so this is a vertical sea-level flight. No flight-time cap.
2. Feasibility: drag-free speed for 5,000 m is sqrt(2 * 9.81 * 5000) = 313 m/s, so the stack needs several thousand newton-seconds once drag is included. An L booster plus a K sustainer is in that ballpark if the airframe stays in the few-kilogram range. Both pieces come down on their own, so each one needs a chute big enough for 4.5 m/s. I want the real class, impulse, diameter and mass before drawing the tubes.
